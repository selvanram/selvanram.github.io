Privacy Policy for Timerpal and Dawn Pages

Effective date: July 22, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to Timerpal and Dawn Pages (collectively, the "Apps"). We ("we", "us", or "our") are committed to protecting your privacy and designed the Apps to work without collecting your personal information.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information, usage data, or content you create in the Apps. In particular, we do not collect Timerpal settings or activity, or writing and journal entries created in Dawn Pages.

3. On-Device Data

App content and settings are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to servers controlled by us. Depending on your device and account settings, Apple services such as device backup may process or store app data. Those services are controlled by Apple and governed by Apple's privacy policy.

4. Cookies and Tracking

The Apps do not use cookies, web beacons, advertising identifiers, or other tracking technologies.

5. Third-Party Services

The Apps do not include third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or other third-party trackers. Apple may process information related to downloading the Apps, purchases, device backups, or diagnostics according to your Apple settings and Apple's privacy policy. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties.

6. Data Retention

Because we do not collect or receive your personal information or app content, we do not retain it on our servers. You control locally stored app data through the Apps and your device.

7. Children's Privacy

The Apps are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security

Keeping app content on your device limits its exposure to us. You are responsible for protecting access to your device and Apple account. No method of electronic storage is completely secure.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Apps or our practices change. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page. If a future version of an App introduces cloud services, analytics, or other data collection, we will update this policy accordingly.
